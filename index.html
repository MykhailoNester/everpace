02 · Train

Hands on the bar. The plan on your wrist.

The watch runs each part for you: the clock, the round, the next movement and the weight. Buttons adjust what you actually did. Heart rate and time are recorded throughout, and the workout goes back to your phone when you finish.

Every set is compared with the last time you did it. Your PR book fills itself from what you actually lifted, and every attempt at a WOD sits side by side.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Do I need a Garmin watch?

No. You can log every workout on the phone; records, history and comparisons work the same. The watch makes it hands-free.

Which phones are supported?

Android, from the Google Play store. An iPhone version is in testing and will follow.

Does it work with my gym's programming?

If you can copy it as text, Everpace can read it: group chats, gym apps, websites or your own notes. Lines it can't read stay as text and can be fixed in two taps.

What happens to my data?

It stays on your phone. Everpace has no accounts and no ads. Crash reports can be turned off, and usage statistics are only sent if you agree. Privacy policy.

What does Pro cost, and how do I cancel?

$19.99 a year or $2.99 a month at the launch price, billed by Google Play, with 14 days free on the yearly plan. Cancel any time in Google Play; Pro stays until the end of what you paid for.
